We desire to equip believers to go to the mission field.

**Local.** We desire to reach the neighborhoods where families at South Point live, as well as the community around the South Point Church campus.

**Regional.** We desire to partner with regional missions organizations to reach communities outside of Kentuckiana for Christ. One of these organizations is a gospel-centered addiction recovery program in Louisville, where we seek to build relationships with those enrolled and teach Scripture in their program.

**Global.** We partner with missions organizations, including Cru and Serge Global, as a way of participating in global gospel advancement. We also prayerfully consider ways to serve on short-term mission trips.
