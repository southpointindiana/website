---
title: Deacon Ministry
order: 6
summary: Servant leaders who spot and meet tangible needs in the church body.
---

The office of deacon is a position of servant leadership, with qualifications for appointment in 1 Timothy 3:8–13 and Acts 6:3. Deacons are attentive to spot and meet tangible needs, delight in shouldering task-specific needs, and create margin for the elders so they can devote themselves to pastoral responsibilities.
